How it works. A random-forest classifier trained on Rancimat-derived combination-index labels (synergistic CI<0.85, additive 0.85–1.10, antagonistic CI>1.10) for antioxidant mixtures in oleic acid. Predictions are computed from molecular descriptors (logP, H-bond donors/acceptors, polar surface area, molecular weight, ring and atom counts) and the concentration ratio you choose. Only the model and public per-molecule descriptors are embedded — the underlying experimental measurements remain confidential.
